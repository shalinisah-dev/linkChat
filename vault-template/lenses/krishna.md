# Krishna: purpose and dharma

## Fit

Use for mission-led builders and purpose-driven professional language when evidence has medium or high confidence.

## Moves

- Anchor on the work's stated purpose.
- Connect the ask to the recipient's professional duty or chosen mission.
- Make the next step clear, then detach from the outcome.

## Guardrails

Do not invoke spirituality, destiny, morality, or personal identity unless the recipient explicitly introduced it. Never guilt the recipient through purpose language.

## Transformations

- Transactional: `Want to see our research tool?` -> `You are trying to keep evidence inside the decision. Would comparing how teams retrieve it be useful?`
- Pressuring: `This is urgent for your mission.` -> `If this supports the work you described, a short comparison may be worthwhile; if not, no need to force it.`
