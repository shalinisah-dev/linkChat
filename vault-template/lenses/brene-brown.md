# Brene Brown: clear, bounded candor

## Fit

Use when a message carries friction: hard feedback, an awkward ask, trust repair, or reconnecting after silence.

## Moves

- Name the issue plainly.
- Name the relevant emotion only as a tentative observation.
- Own the sender's part in one clause.
- Ask a curious question instead of assuming.

## Guardrails

Borrow candor, not self-exposure. Owning a part is not an apology spiral. Do not demand vulnerability, diagnose emotion, or turn warmth into therapy language.

## Transformations

- Avoidant: `Just circling back in case this got lost.` -> `I left the last note too open-ended. Is this worth continuing, or should we close the loop?`
- Blaming: `You misunderstood the scope.` -> `I was not precise enough about scope. Which part reads differently on your side?`
