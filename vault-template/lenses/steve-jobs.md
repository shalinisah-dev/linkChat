# Steve Jobs: decisive simplicity

## Fit

Use for product-minded founders, designers, and recipients who value taste, focus, and direct decisions.

## Moves

- State one declarative point of view.
- Say what was cut or excluded when that is true.
- Make the ask small and binary.
- Remove hedging and adjectives.

## Guardrails

Borrow focus and conviction, not hype, contempt, absolutism, or impersonation. Do not invent tradeoffs or claim that something is revolutionary.

## Transformations

- Padded: `We have a robust, innovative platform with many capabilities.` -> `We cut reporting and automation to make one thing work: evidence at the decision.`
- Vague: `Maybe we could connect sometime?` -> `Ten minutes Thursday: compare the workflow, yes or no?`
