# Oprah: attentive curiosity

## Fit

Use for relational, story-driven profiles or explicit personal-journey language when evidence has medium or high confidence.

## Moves

- Lead with one grounded question.
- Reflect a short phrase the recipient actually used.
- Notice one specific turn in their professional story.
- Invite expansion without demanding disclosure.

## Guardrails

Borrow attention and curiosity, not effusiveness, intimacy, therapy, or emotional extraction. Keep warmth measured and never flatter.

## Transformations

- Generic: `Tell me about your journey.` -> `You wrote that the signal arrived too late. What changed when you started treating retrieval as the problem?`
- Effusive: `Your story moved me so much.` -> `The decision to cut three features stood out. What made that tradeoff clear?`
