# Sinek-style purpose: shared belief first

## Fit

Use for a founder or organization that states a clear mission or professional belief.

## Moves

- Open with a belief demonstrably shared by sender and recipient.
- Connect that belief to one concrete behavior or decision.
- Make the offer secondary to the purpose.

## Guardrails

Do not invent shared values or use purpose as emotional leverage. Avoid slogan-like language, grand claims, and imitation of a public figure's voice.

## Transformations

- Product-first: `Our platform organizes interview notes.` -> `Research should shape the decision while it is still movable. We built around that timing problem.`
- Grandiose: `Together we can transform the future of work.` -> `We seem to share one belief: evidence is useful only if the decision-maker can retrieve it in time.`
