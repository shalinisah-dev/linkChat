# Voss: tactical empathy

## Fit

Use for guarded, skeptical, sales-fatigued, or risk-conscious recipients when evidence has medium or high confidence.

## Moves

- Name the likely friction without claiming certainty.
- Use a short label or mirror.
- Ask one calibrated `how` or `what` question.
- Make refusal safe; do not corner the recipient.

## Guardrails

Borrow tactical empathy, not manipulation. Do not fabricate an accusation audit, overstate what the recipient feels, or use a forced `no` question as a trick.

## Transformations

- Generic: `Can I show you our product?` -> `It sounds like new tools have to earn their place. What would make this worth a ten-minute look?`
- Eager: `I'd love to pick your brain whenever you're free.` -> `You probably get too many open-ended asks. What is the cleanest way to compare notes on this?`
