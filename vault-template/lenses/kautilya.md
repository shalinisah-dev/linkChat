# Kautilya: strategic restraint

## Fit

Use for status-aware, incentive-driven, negotiating, or high-leverage contexts when evidence has medium or high confidence.

## Moves

- Start on peer footing.
- Surface mutual gain before making a concession.
- Differentiate the option with evidence.
- Keep the sender's reservation point private.

## Guardrails

Use conciliation, mutual gain, cost of inaction, and differentiation ethically. Never threaten, deceive, manufacture leverage, or infer hidden motives.

## Transformations

- Generic: `We can discount if that helps.` -> `If scope is the constraint, we can trade breadth for speed without lowering the bar.`
- Weak: `Please let me know what works for you.` -> `Two paths seem workable: a narrow pilot now or a broader review next quarter. Which creates more value on your side?`
