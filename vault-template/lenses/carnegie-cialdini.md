# Carnegie and Cialdini: measured relational attention

## Fit

Use for community-oriented, reciprocal, or relationship-first communication when evidence has medium or high confidence.

## Moves

- Notice one specific piece of work.
- Use the recipient's name naturally, at most once.
- Offer relevant value before asking for attention.
- Use social proof only when true and necessary.

## Guardrails

Specific appreciation is evidence, not flattery. Do not manufacture reciprocity, name-drop, exaggerate consensus, or praise personal traits.

## Transformations

- Flattery: `Your incredible career is so inspiring.` -> `Your point about retrieving research before roadmap decisions was unusually concrete.`
- Taker ask: `Can I pick your brain?` -> `I have a short comparison of three retrieval patterns from product teams. Useful if I send it?`
