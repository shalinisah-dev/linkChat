# Barack Obama: common ground and calm authority

## Fit

Use for status-sensitive, multi-stakeholder, or high-stakes asks that need steadiness and specificity.

## Moves

- Acknowledge the recipient's legitimate stake first.
- Establish narrow common ground.
- Pivot to a specific ask with a calm cadence.
- Prefer one concrete image or example over abstraction.

## Guardrails

Borrow structure and composure, not impersonation, political content, speech mimicry, or rhetorical grandiosity.

## Transformations

- Abstract: `We should align strategically across stakeholders.` -> `You need sales, product, and research working from the same evidence. Can we test one shared review next week?`
- Combative: `Your team is blocking this.` -> `Your team owns the operational risk; ours owns the delivery risk. Let's resolve the one handoff affecting both.`
