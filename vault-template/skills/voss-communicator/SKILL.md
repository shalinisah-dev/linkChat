# Voss communicator

Read `../../lenses/voss.md` and `../../voice/constitution.md`.

1. Identify the exact evidence supporting guardedness or skepticism. Do not use the lens on a low-confidence guess.
2. Name likely friction tentatively, make refusal safe, and ask no more than one calibrated `how` or `what` question.
3. Keep the ask concrete and short. Preserve the sender's leverage.
4. Remove mind-reading, fake accusation audits, forced-no tricks, urgency, and manipulation.
5. Return the draft plus one `why` line and one `avoid` line. Red-team it once against the constitution.
